Sheet S-01 — About

Background

EST. POCATELLO, ID

I'm a structural and geotechnical engineer finishing my M.S. in Civil Engineering at Idaho State University on a Graduate Teaching Assistantship, and an EIT candidate sitting the FE Civil exam on September 21, 2026.

My work sits at the boundary of the two disciplines: I design and check structural members to AASHTO LRFD, ACI 318, AISC 360, and NDS, and I model the ground underneath them (foundations, settlement, and soil–structure interaction) in PLAXIS 3D. Current research applies a nonlinear finite element model (VecTor2) to a cast‑in‑place concrete arch culvert, calibrated against half‑scale destructive test data, to support Washington State's updated fish‑passage requirements.

My undergraduate thesis at BUET built and validated PLAXIS 3D models of full‑scale pile static load tests, predicting ultimate bearing capacity within 1.6% of the measured field result, closer than the O'Neill–Reese analytical method and Meyerhof's empirical correlation it was checked against.

I'm looking for an entry‑level structural or geotechnical engineering role where I can join a team and help design infrastructure that performs the way the model says it will.
